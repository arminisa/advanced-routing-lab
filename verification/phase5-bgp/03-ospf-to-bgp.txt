PHASE 5 - BGP - 03: REDISTRIBUTION (OSPF → BGP)
================================================================
Project: advanced-routing-lab
Date: 2026-10-08

----------------------------------------------------------------
CONFIGURATION - R1-Core
----------------------------------------------------------------

router bgp 65001
 redistribute ospf 1 match internal external 1 external 2

----------------------------------------------------------------
KEY CONFIG NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------

Command breakdown:
- "internal"      : Intra-area (O) and Inter-area (O IA) routes
- "external 1"    : OSPF E1 routes
- "external 2"    : OSPF E2 routes

WHY ALL THREE?
EIGRP-derived routes (from R-ASBR) come into OSPF as E1/E2.
Without "external 1 external 2", these routes would NOT be
redistributed into BGP → R-ISP can't reach EIGRP networks.
